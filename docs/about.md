# About the project

JupyterHealth is an open-source project that brings Jupyter's tools and community to health care.
It aims to connect data that patients manage themselves with their clinical team, and to give clinicians access to a modern data analytics stack through Jupyter.
[jupyterhealth.org](https://jupyterhealth.org) is the source of truth for the project's vision, use cases, and team.
This page is a short summary with pointers.

## Origins and governance

JupyterHealth started at UC Berkeley and UCSF as part of the Agile Metabolic Health initiative, with [The Commons Project](https://www.thecommonsproject.org), [2i2c](https://2i2c.org), and [Project Jupyter](https://jupyter.org).
It is governed as a [JupyterHub subproject](https://github.com/jupyterhub/team-compass/issues/752).
The team is listed on the [team page](https://jupyterhealth.org/team/).
